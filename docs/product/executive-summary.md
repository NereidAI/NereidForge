## In One Sentence

**NereidForge is a model factory deployed in the customer's own data center. It enables a small team of three to five people to continuously turn business images and videos into AI models that run in real time on field devices—without data leaving the premises, with assets retained in-house, and with accelerating returns over time.**

## Three Barriers Facing Customers

1. **Data cannot leave the premises**: Compliance requirements in industry, government, security, and finance rule out cloud services for sensitive data.
2. **Talent is unaffordable and difficult to retain**: AI projects depend on a small number of experts manually connecting every step, so expertise resides with individuals and cannot be replicated.
3. **Models cannot reach the field**: Models perform well in the lab but will not run, or run fast enough, on inexpensive edge devices and domestic chips.

## Our Answer: From a Manual Workshop to a Production Line

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| Every project starts from scratch and takes quarters | Data → annotation → training → quality inspection → compression → deployment in one production line, with delivery measured in weeks |
| Annotation relies entirely on manual labor, including frame-by-frame video bounding boxes | **Machines complete roughly 60% of the work first, while people review and correct**; for video, people annotate only key frames and machines track objects automatically |
| Models are scattered across personal computers | Data, models, and performance reports are registered centrally and fully traceable, so assets remain when people leave |
| Every chip requires a separate adaptation, creating hardware lock-in | Train once and automatically produce models adapted to multiple hardware types, including domestic platforms |
| AI is unavailable when fully disconnected | The system continues to operate while completely disconnected, with even foundation models preinstalled |

## The Value Flywheel

```
More data → more accurate models → less annotation labor → more use cases for the same team
```

The annotation team remains stable in size while business use cases continue to expand, so labor costs no longer grow linearly with data volume.

## Verifiable Figures (No Unsupported Promises)

- Internal test (128 street-scene images, 8 object classes): one preannotation run **automatically produced 269 detection boxes across 75 images**, meaning approximately 59% of images did not require annotation from scratch.
- We recommend measuring these targets in the first pilot use case: **at least 50% fewer annotation hours, delivery time reduced to one-third, and ordinary engineers able to operate independently within two weeks**.
- The whitepaper includes a standard ROI calculation table; enter the customer's own data to quantify annual savings.

## Suitable Customers

- Industries with sensitive data, real-time field requirements, and large video volumes, including **manufacturing quality inspection, transportation and security, energy inspection, government and defense, and campus and property management**.
- **AI integrators** delivering solutions to these industries, using the platform as a standardized foundation to reduce the marginal cost of each project.

**Not suitable for**: organizations that use only cloud-based LLM APIs and have no proprietary data feedback loop, or organizations running only one or two pilots per year with no plans to scale.

## Recommended Next Step

Select a use case with a solid data foundation and clear business value for a **four-to-eight-week joint pilot**. Validate the three quantified targets above using the customer's real data, then replicate the proven approach across additional use cases.
