Kubeflow platform deployed via the `kubeflow` umbrella helm chart (16 subcharts):
  15 upstream components: cert-manager, istio, kubeflow-namespace, kubeflow-roles,
    dex, oauth2-proxy, kubeflow-istio-resources, knative, kfp, katib, kserve,
    dashboard, notebooks, trainer, workspaces
  1 platform-config: tenant Profile, Model Registry, auth RBAC, internal CA, istio caBundle

Each component is an independent helm subchart driven by a post-install/upgrade
hook Job (weighted by .Values.hookWeight for correct apply order). Manage them
individually with helm:
  helm history nereidforge-kubeflow -n kubeflow
  helm rollback nereidforge-kubeflow <revision> -n kubeflow

Watch the install hooks:
  kubectl get jobs -n kubeflow
  kubectl logs -n kubeflow job/nereidforge-kubeflow-install-<component> -f
